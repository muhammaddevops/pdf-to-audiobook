1. **On the voluntary prayers**

Abu Hurayrah (ؓ) reported: The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) encouraged praying before the Zuhr prayer, and said that whoever keeps to it will find it a means of good in this life and the next.

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

This narration is reported in Sunan al-Tirmidhī, where it is graded Ṣaḥīḥ. The scholars said that the one who recites the Qurʾān after Fajr and remembers Allah until sunrise, then prays 2 cycles, has completed a great act of worship in Islam.
